Summary
Traffic grew 12% month on month, driven almost entirely by the checkout API following the February

pricing launch. Latency improved despite the extra load, thanks to the connection-pool change shipped
on the 8th.

One incident affected search for 41 minutes on 19 February. Root cause was an unbounded query intro­
duced by a third-party integration; a query timeout has since been added.



Actions for next month

Reporting remains the slowest service at a p95 of 884 ms. The team will move its aggregate queries onto
the read replica and revisit the index on the events table.

Error rate rose slightly, entirely within the webhooks service. The retry policy will be changed from fixed

to exponential backoff.
